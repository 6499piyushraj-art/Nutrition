What can we estimate?

Gemini may estimate calcium, iron, magnesium, potassium, zinc, vitamins C and D, B12, sodium and omega-3 where the food description supports it. Blank means unknown—not zero.

Micronutrients are often harder to estimate than calories and macros. This screen only totals values returned for logged meals. Sulfur is not separately estimated; protein-containing foods are a qualitative proxy. An unreported nutrient is unknown, not zero.
